How AgriRAG works

  • Question: typed or spoken
  • Embed: query and knowledge entries become weighted term vectors
  • Retrieve: cosine similarity ranks entries, crop match adds a boost
  • Augment: top 3 entries form the grounded context
  • Advise: context becomes action steps with cited sources

This demo runs in the browser with TF-IDF cosine retrieval. The project notebook uses sentence-transformers embeddings, and an LLM plugs into the Advise step for production. Doses are indicative; confirm with your local agriculture officer.
